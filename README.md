## Showcase

### AegisFlow

**AegisFlow** is a gated, evidence-driven AI development governance framework for AI-native product development.

It helps teams use AI to accelerate product planning, solution design, architecture decisions, spike validation, and delivery preparation—without allowing AI to bypass human review, evidence requirements, or release governance.

**Positioning**

- A gated, evidence-driven AI development governance framework
- An auditable AI-native product development workflow
- AI acceleration with governance

**Key ideas**

- Human review gates for critical decisions
- Evidence matrices and risk registers for traceability
- Clear separation between planning, solution design, implementation readiness, and release readiness
- Guardrails against unsupported AI-generated claims
- Structured workflows for PRD, UX, Legal/Privacy/Security, Architecture, ADRs, Spikes, and downstream delivery
